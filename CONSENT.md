# Consent and what happens to the data

This is the notice shown on the join screen, in the same words.

## What this room records

This room records what happens at the table: every move of a card or piece, every vote, any text you type into the table, and the display name you join with. It never records audio or video; the call you are on is separate.

The Ethics Sandbox is being playtested. The people running the playtest at the Edmond & Lily Safra Center for Ethics, Harvard University, read the record to see how the exercises work and how to improve them.

Your facilitator can use the record to help your group talk about what happened. A copy can be exported with names replaced by pseudonyms and typed text left out, and it is used that way in any write-up.

A room goes dormant after 12 hours without activity. Its record is deleted 30 days after the last activity. Your facilitator can delete it sooner.

To ask for your record to be deleted, ask your facilitator, or write to the R&D Studio at the Center and give the room code.
